IGNFT INNOVATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🌟 Key Innovation: Discussion rooms as Interoperable Generative NFTs

What is IGNFT?
  • Living artifacts that evolve with community feedback
  • Content can be blocked and chained for versioning
  • Morphous standard adapting to creative workflows
  • Foundation for Web3/blockchain integration

Benefits:
  • Authors maintain ownership and attribution
  • Readers verify authenticity
  • Collaborators get credit
  • Cross-platform content sharing
  • Ready for Web3 monetization
